Introduction to Local Video Models: Minimax H3

Every component and setting has a specific function that we will dissect together in this clear and visual guide.

1

The Big Picture

Read this first

H3 does not "film" anything. It starts with a screen of pure static and cleans it up a little at a time until it looks (and sounds) like your prompt. Picture and sound are generated together in the same pass, which is why lips sync with the voice.

You

Prompt

Your words, plus pictures, clips or audio depending on the mode.

The Reader

Text Encoder

Reads the prompt and translates it into numbers. (Load CLIP)

The Artist

Video Model

Cleans up the static step by step guided by the reader. Nearly all of your wait is here.

The Unpackers

Video & Audio VAE

Turns the compressed version back into real frames and real sound.

Result

MP4 File

24 frames per second, stereo sound.

start: static
step 4
step 8
step 12
step 16
step 20: done

A step is one cleanup pass. More passes mean a cleaner and more detailed result, but a longer wait.

The Modes

What you plug in

The letters only describe what goes in. The VA at the end means Video + Audio comes out. Every mode makes sound.

ModeYou plug inWhat H3 does with itReach for it when...
T2VA
Text to video		 A prompt. Nothing else. Invents everything: the people, the place, the camera, the sound. You have no starting picture. Total freedom, minimal visual control.
I2VA
Image to video		 A prompt and 1 picture (first_frame). Your picture becomes the opening frame. The video advances from it. You already have the exact look you want and need it to move/talk.
FL2VA
First & Last frame		 A prompt, 1 start picture, 1 end picture. Opens on picture 1, lands on picture 2, and invents the transition between them. The ending has to hit an exact image (e.g., before/after). Describe the journey, not the pictures.
L2VA
Last frame		 A prompt and 1 end picture. Invents a beginning to arrive at your picture. You want a reveal that ends on one specific shot.
Ref2VA
Reference to video		 A prompt and references (up to 9 pictures, 3 videos, 3 audios). Treats them as examples (face, voice, motion), not as actual animation frames. You want to place a specific character or voice in a BRAND NEW scene.
3

The Pieces of the Puzzle

What the files are for

A workflow loads 4 model files. Each one has its own node on the canvas.

Diffusion Model The Video Model

a.k.a: checkpoint, UNet, DiT

Its job: Creates the video and sound. This is THE central model.

Its load in the process: It is the biggest file. This is where you choose the global mode (fl2va or ref2va file).

Text Encoder The Reader

a.k.a: CLIP, qwen3-vl

Its job: Reads your prompt/images, translates everything into numbers the video model understands.

Its load in the process: Massive AI model (32 billion parameters). It runs once per prompt.

Video VAE Image Unpacker

Its job: The video model works on a compressed version. The VAE decompresses everything at the end to create the final frames.

Its load in the process: Demands a huge VRAM memory spike at the very end. If it crashes after 100%, this is often why.

Audio VAE Sound Unpacker

Its job: Same thing, but turns the video model's data into 32 kHz stereo sound.

Its load in the process: Very little. Small file, rarely causes issues.

Other common terms

LoRA: A "patch" grafted onto the video model to force a style, a face, or to speed it up (Turbo).
Example of a LoRA: A Turbo LoRA speeds up the process (8 steps instead of 30) at the expense of some quality.

Decoding Names

Understanding the letters
minimax_h3_ fl2va_ pruned_ int8_convrot .safetensors

The Magic Number (Formats)

A model is a giant list of numbers. These letters indicate how many bits are used to store each number. Fewer bits = smaller file, fits better on your card, slightly less accurate result.

bf1616 bits per number. The original: max quality, huge.
int88 bits. Half the size of bf16.
convrotA math trick that makes 8-bit high quality.
fp8_scaledThe older 8-bit format. Use if int8 crashes for you.
w6a86 bits stored, 8 bits active. Smallest official diffusion model.
nvfp4NVIDIA 4-bit format. Official text encoder uses this (14.6 GB).
awqThe 4-bit rounding method to avoid quality loss.
pruned~40% smaller with no major loss. Recommended version.
fp16 / fp32Classic storage. Used on VAEs since they are already small.

The Settings

The Video Model's knobs

Change only one thing at a time with the seed fixed, otherwise you won't know what caused the effect.

SettingWhat it actually doesStarting Value
Resolution
Megapixels (MP)		 Sharpness and detail. Higher means exponentially more VRAM consumption. H3 was trained for 1 MP max. 0.4 MP (864x480)
Go up if it runs well.
Duration
Seconds / Frames		 Clip duration (always 24 fps). Longer means each step takes more time and VRAM. 5 seconds
FPS
Frames per second		 H3 ALWAYS creates 24 frames for each second of sound. If changed afterward, sound will desync. 24 (Do not touch)
Seed The initial static "noise" pattern. Fix it to test settings. Set to random for new takes. Fixed (for testing)
Steps Cleanup passes. More passes, sharper image and motion (time scales proportionally). 20 (12 to 16 for testing)
Sampler / Scheduler The method and pacing used to clean the noise. res_multistep / simple
Turbo (LoRA Lightning) Speeds up process to finish in 8 steps instead of 20 (Great for drafts, loses detail). Disabled for final

Hardware and Offloading

Why is it slow?

H3 is bigger than your graphics card, and that's normal. It works anyway, just a bit slower thanks to a mechanic called Offloading.

The Workbench

VRAM (20 GB)

Graphics card memory. Whatever is on it runs at top speed. This is the bottleneck.

The Shelf

RAM (64 GB)

PC memory. Whatever doesn't fit on the workbench waits here. Close your Chrome tabs!

The Shuffle

Offloading

ComfyUI automatically moves pieces between the shelf and workbench at the right time. It costs time.

Troubleshooting (Too slow or VRAM error)

  1. Lower Megapixels: Drop to 0.8 or 0.9 instead of 1 for longer videos. It's the most massive saving.
    Note: Dropping from 1 MP (1,000,000 pixels) to 0.9 MP (900,000 pixels) saves 100,000 pixels per frame. Dropping to 0.8 MP (800,000 pixels) saves another 100,000. Over a 120-frame video (5s), 0.8 MP spares the calculation of 24 million pixels compared to 1 MP!
  2. Shorten the clip: Drop back to 5 seconds.
  3. Lower Steps: Drop to 16, or use Turbo (8 steps). (Saves time).
  4. Use smaller files: Grab the pruned_w6a8 model and the int8_convrot Video VAE.
  5. Free the PC: Close all other applications. If PC RAM (64 GB) is full, the PC freezes.
  6. "CUDA out of memory" error: The workbench overflowed. Apply points 1, 2, and 4.

Troubleshooting and Glossary

Understanding errors

What an error is telling you

Red boxes on opening
Missing custom nodes, or ComfyUI needs an update. Update first, then use ComfyUI Manager.
"Value not in list"
The file is not in the correct folder (models/...), or doesn't have the exact expected name.
"CUDA out of memory"
The graphics card (VRAM) is full. Lower resolution, reduce duration.
Dies AFTER the last step
Usually the video VAE running out of memory while unpacking the final video.
Whole PC freezes or crawls
PC RAM (64 GB) is full and Windows is using the hard drive. Use smaller models (w6a8).

Other domain words

The Plumbing
CUDA / PyTorch: PyTorch is ComfyUI's math engine. CUDA is the NVIDIA layer allowing the use of the graphics card. You never touch them, unless the log mentions it needs cu130.
An add-on
Custom Node: Every box on screen is a "node". A Custom Node is created by the community. H3 doesn't need any (they are native).
A popular pack
rgthree: A set of convenience nodes (like switches to toggle workflow parts on/off). Very often required online.

Creation Exercise

Your first mini movie

Put your new knowledge into practice.

Exercise Instructions

"A character climbs out the window, jumps into a vehicle, and speeds away"

Put this script into a prompt by choosing all the shots, all the details: What style? (film, anime, 50s cartoon, ...), what character? (not necessarily human), what clothes? (Police officer, detective, salesperson, ...), what type of vehicle? (car, bike, helicopter, ...), ... Be original, I don't want to see the same thing twice!

Don't forget to give information about what you want to get visually, but also on the sound level.

Writing the Prompt

H3 creates image AND sound at the same time.

In the same block, describe: what is seen, what is said, and what is heard (the look, camera movements, exact dialogs, music, background noises).

The AI understands long and precise descriptions very well.

Basic rule of image/video prompting:

Imagine that you are describing a scene precisely to a blind person.
